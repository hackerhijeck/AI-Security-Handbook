# AI Vendor / Third-Party Assessment Questionnaire

## General
- Which models are used (own/third-party)? Versions and change notification?
- Certifications: ISO 27001, ISO 42001, SOC 2?

## Data
- Is our data used to train models? Can we opt out contractually?
- Retention period for prompts/outputs/logs? Zero-retention option?
- Data location/regions; subprocessors list
- Encryption in transit/at rest; tenant isolation

## Security
- Prompt injection and jailbreak testing performed? Results shared?
- Pen test / red team frequency
- Access control, SSO, audit logs
- Vulnerability disclosure and patching SLAs
- Supply chain: model provenance, dependency scanning

## Compliance
- EU AI Act role and classification; documentation available?
- GDPR/DPDP: DPA, breach notification timeline
- Sector-specific requirements

## Operations
- Uptime SLA, incident notification timeline
- Rate limits and cost controls
- Human oversight features; explainability
- Exit plan: data return/deletion

## Contract clauses to consider
No training on customer data, audit rights, incident notice, IP indemnity, subprocessor approval, liability, termination assistance.
