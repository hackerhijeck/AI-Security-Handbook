# Acceptable Use of AI: Policy Template

**Purpose:** Allow safe, lawful and ethical use of AI tools.
**Scope:** All employees, contractors, and systems using AI.

## Rules
1. Use only **approved AI tools** for company work.
2. Do **not** enter confidential, personal, regulated, or source-code data into unapproved tools.
3. **Verify** AI outputs before use; you are accountable for the result.
4. Do not use AI for prohibited purposes (deception, harassment, discrimination, illegal activity).
5. Disclose AI use where required (customers, regulators).
6. Report suspected incidents (data leak, strange behavior) to security immediately.
7. New AI use cases require risk assessment and approval.
8. Agents/automation need defined permissions and human oversight for high-impact actions.

## Roles
Owners, approvers, security, privacy, employees.

## Enforcement
Violations may lead to disciplinary action. Review this policy at least annually.
