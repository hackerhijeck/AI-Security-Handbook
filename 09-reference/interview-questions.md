# AI Security Interview Questions

1. What is prompt injection? Direct vs indirect? Why is it hard to fix?
2. Walk through the OWASP LLM Top 10 and prioritize for a RAG chatbot.
3. How does the Agentic Top 10 differ from the LLM Top 10?
4. Explain least agency and give an example.
5. How do you secure a RAG system's access control?
6. What are risks of MCP servers and how to mitigate?
7. Difference between data poisoning and model poisoning?
8. How would you red-team an LLM agent with email access?
9. Explain NIST AI RMF functions.
10. What is ISO 42001 and how does it relate to ISO 27001?
11. How does the EU AI Act classify risk? What are provider obligations for high-risk AI?
12. What is an AI-BOM and why does it matter?
13. How do you detect and respond to a hijacked agent?
14. How do you handle shadow AI?
15. How to balance AI innovation with compliance?

## Model answer tip
Structure: define, give example, impact, controls, how to test.
