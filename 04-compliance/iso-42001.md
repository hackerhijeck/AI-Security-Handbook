# ISO/IEC 42001:2023 (AI Management System)

First international **certifiable** standard for an **AI Management System (AIMS)**. Structure follows ISO harmonized structure (like ISO 27001).

## Clauses 4 to 10 (requirements)
| Clause | Topic | Example output |
|---|---|---|
| 4 | Context of the organization | Scope, interested parties, AI role |
| 5 | Leadership | AI policy, roles and responsibilities |
| 6 | Planning | AI risk assessment, AI impact assessment, objectives |
| 7 | Support | Resources, competence, awareness, documented information |
| 8 | Operation | Operational controls, lifecycle management |
| 9 | Performance evaluation | Monitoring, internal audit, management review |
| 10 | Improvement | Nonconformity, corrective action |

## Annex A controls (themes)
- Policies related to AI
- Internal organization
- Resources for AI systems (data, tooling, human)
- Assessing impacts of AI systems
- AI system lifecycle
- Data for AI systems
- Information for interested parties
- Use of AI systems
- Third-party and customer relationships

Annex B gives implementation guidance. Related: **ISO/IEC 23894** (AI risk management guidance), **ISO/IEC 27001** (security), **ISO/IEC 27701** (privacy), **ISO/IEC 5338** (AI lifecycle).

## Implementation roadmap
1. Define scope and AI role (developer, provider, user).
2. Gap analysis.
3. Establish AI policy and governance.
4. AI inventory, risk and impact assessments.
5. Select controls; write Statement of Applicability.
6. Implement and document.
7. Internal audit and management review.
8. Certification audit (Stage 1, Stage 2) by an accredited body.

## ISO 42001 vs NIST AI RMF
| | ISO 42001 | NIST AI RMF |
|---|---|---|
| Nature | Certifiable standard | Voluntary framework |
| Focus | Management system | Risk process/outcomes |
| Use together? | Yes, NIST gives detailed risk practices; ISO gives auditable structure |
